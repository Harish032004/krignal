Krignal is a curated global business network built around three connected layers: Krignal TV for discovery, Krignal Events for meaningful in-person rooms, and Krignal AI for context-based introductions.

Krignal TV features Brand Stories, Journeys That Inspire, Get to Know profiles, Find Your Person connection stories and Week’s Insights. Celebrity and leadership conversations can also appear as special programming.

Access depends on the event. Some rooms are open to relevant applicants, while others are Invite Only, Off the Record or reserved for Internal Members. Every gathering is curated around a specific industry or business intent.

Your AI twin understands your skills, interests and current intent. It exchanges relevant context with another member’s AI twin and recommends a human introduction only when both sides appear meaningfully aligned.

No. AI supports discovery, filtering and context exchange. Members always decide whether to meet, collaborate or move forward. Trust and final decisions remain human.

Membership begins with an application or referral. Krignal reviews relevance, intent and potential contribution to the network before confirming individual or corporate membership.
